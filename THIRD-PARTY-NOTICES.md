`deploy/fedora/install-native.sh` also installs:

- Fedora packages: PostgreSQL, TeX Live, Poppler, qpdf, Ghostscript, restic,
  bubblewrap, Qt 6 and KDE Frameworks development files and others, each under its
  own licence;
- `thoughts-comment-sync`, built from the matching Thoughts desktop checkout
  (GPL-2.0-or-later).

`clients/thoughts-cli/` uses only the Python standard library. The tests of
`clients/html-viewer/` use Playwright (Apache-2.0) when it is available; the
Kotlin SDK (`sdk/kotlin/`) builds with Gson 2.14.0 (Apache-2.0) and OkHttp 4.12.0
(Apache-2.0) and tests with JUnit 4.13.2 (EPL-1.0), as declared in its
`build.gradle.kts`. The C++ SDK (`sdk/cpp/`) builds against Qt 6 Core, Network and
Test (LGPL-3.0-only or GPL-2.0-or-later or GPL-3.0-only, <https://www.qt.io/>).
